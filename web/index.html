FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got Questions? We Have Answers.

Will Mint-Island slow down my laptop or drain battery?

Absolutely not. Mint-Island was specifically built for users migrating to Linux Mint seeking a lightweight, responsive system. Because it is written natively in Python with GTK4 and Libadwaita, it uses event-driven D-Bus listeners rather than polling loops. Its idle CPU usage is 0.0%, and memory consumption stays at a rock-solid ~45 MB RAM.

Does it work with dual monitors or external HDMI displays?

Yes! Mint-Island has full dynamic multi-monitor support. From the Control Panel GUI, you can choose whether the island displays on your Primary HDMI monitor or your laptop's eDP screen. It repositioning happens on the fly with X11 window hints.

How does synchronized karaoke lyrics work?

When playing music on YouTube (in Brave, Chrome, Firefox) or Spotify, Mint-Island captures track metadata via MPRIS D-Bus. It cleans video title noise (e.g., removing "[Official 4K Video]") and queries LRCLIB API for exact timestamps. Lyrics are cached locally in ~/.cache/mint-island/lyrics/ for instant offline access.
